★ Automated run

After step 1 below has recorded a collection search and a deck, this runs the remaining tests in one go: request contexts, deck printing data, collection search page-size limit, the deckId filter, batched OR lookups, a rate test, and the deck list and folder layouts. To test a specific deck, put its id in section 4 or open this page from a 🥒 button. Otherwise it uses the last deck you opened.


    
Round 2: after the automated run, this tests how to look up cards in collection search: single exact-name searches, several OR syntaxes, and 6 lookups at a time.

    

    

    
Round 3: in Moxfield, search your collection for !"Evolving Wilds" and click Refresh in step 1. This replays that exact search, then changes one thing at a time to find why the probe's searches return fewer results than the site's.

    

    

    
Round 4: can the extension make up its own searchSessionId? This uses the last recorded collection search and deck (or the deck id in section 4).

    

    

    
Round 5: with the probe reloaded, open your Moxfield collection page and search for a card you haven't searched yet, e.g. !"Hero of Bladehold". Then click Refresh in step 1, then this button. It searches Moxfield's own scripts for how searchSessionId is built, lists the recent traffic to every host, and compares new searches made from the extension page and from the content script.

    

    

    
Round 6: search the way the site does. Re-request with the server's searchSessionId until hasMore is false, then check that every card in the deck is found.

    

    

    
Round 7: split the deck into groups of names, search the groups 4 at a time, and find the per-query limit.

    

    

    
Optional: tests whether the extension can get its own access token. This rotates your Moxfield session token, which is harmless but could, at worst, sign you out of Moxfield.
